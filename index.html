01 / About me

The short version

Fifteen years ago, I started as an apprentice at a B2B agency in a small Bavarian town. I finished top of my year at the Regensburg Chamber of Commerce.

Since then: brands built, shops relaunched, a startup co-founded, countless logos made bigger, and three years of freelance web design. That last part taught me everything design school politely leaves out — pricing, saying no without saying “no”, and what a client actually means when they say “make it pop.”

I've worked across most corners of design. Digital has always been my favourite.
