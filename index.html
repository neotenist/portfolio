The short version

Fifteen years ago I started as an apprentice at a B2B agency in a small rural town in Bavaria and finished top of my year at the Regensburg Chamber of Commerce.

Since then: brands built, shops relaunched, a startup co‑founded, countless logos made bigger, and three years of web design freelancing. That last part taught me everything design school politely leaves out — pricing, saying no without saying "No", and what a client actually means when they say "make it pop."

There is barely a space in design I haven't touched, but I've always had a special place in my heart for digital design.
