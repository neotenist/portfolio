Dr. Eva Elisa Schneider is a psychologist and psychotherapist who works with companies on mental health: helping organisations understand the subject, take it seriously and actually act on it. She came to me without a brand, so we started at the beginning.

I built the corporate design from scratch and the website with it, from positioning through the visual identity to parts of the copy. A brand around mental health in a corporate setting has to be credible to an HR director and approachable to the employee reading it.

One of my favourite projects from my freelance years. It ran smoothly from the first conversation to launch, and the site is still online today, unchanged since 2023.
