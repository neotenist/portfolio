Direct Mail Interactive

A brochure about brochures. The category needed pushing, and the honest way to sell paper is to put it in someone's hands.

We used several different paper stocks in one brochure, which is unusual enough to be a talking point in itself. The finishing options are demonstrated rather than listed, starting with a cover finished twice over. Between them sit quizzes, crosswords and did-you-know panels, so the reader works through the information instead of being lectured at.

Every page is designed differently, in a scrapbook style built from stickers and torn-paper elements. The handmade look is deliberate. It builds a bridge to the craft behind the printing.
