The receipts.

Shapes'n'Calendars

Client
de-Agentur (charity project)
Year
2015
Role
Concept, design
Focus
Print

The photographers and the printing house behind this project produce a charity calendar together every year, and for each edition they bring in a different agency to design it. That year the choice fell on ours, and I got the job as the junior designer on the team. It went on to win my first award.

The idea was to let the photography set the rules. For each month, a shape from inside the image gets picked up and rebuilt as a graphic element on the page below it. Twelve months, twelve photographs, twelve different shapes to find.

The hard part was finding a shape in every single photograph that was strong enough to carry a whole page.

Results

  • German Design Award, Special Mention 2015
