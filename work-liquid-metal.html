A WebGL shader that behaves like liquid metal — dark chrome, catching and bending light as it moves.

Speed, scale, distortion, how brushed the metal reads, how metallic versus matte it feels, and the base, highlight and mid tones are all parameter-driven in real time. There's no pre-rendered version of this — what's running above is the actual shader.

It took a few real passes to look right — an early version read more like a distorted mirror than metal, so the lighting got reworked until it actually looked like chrome.
