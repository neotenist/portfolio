The receipts.

A mailing that doesn't get binned

Client
Onlineprinters
Year
2025
Role
Concept, design, copy
Focus
Print, Direct mail

Most B2B mailings have one job and fail it: surviving the walk from the post room to the desk. I set a harder target. Make something that stays on the break room table.

So the mailing stopped being a mailing and became a game. Inside is a round of Stadt-Land-Fluss, a German category game, printed onto one of our own notepads, so the product doubles as the board. A branded pen to play with. Two bags of gummy bears, one for the winner and one as a consolation prize. Stickers for both outcomes, because losing deserves a sticker too.

What I wanted was for people to actually use the thing and spend a few minutes with our branding, rather than glance at it once.
